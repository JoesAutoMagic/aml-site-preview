Your Signal, Your Store

If 95% of your buyers are on your website before purchase, why isn't that your key signal?

Most inventory tools react to market conditions from the outside. TurnSignal starts with what your shoppers are already doing on your website, one VIN at a time. That creates a signal that is closer to your store, closer to your inventory, and closer to real buyer behavior.

Hot VINs. Cold VINs.

TurnSignal separates hot VINs from cold VINs.

Hot VINs

They get attention early. They get shopper traffic early. They show signs that say: do not drop the price.

Cold VINs

They get little attention. They show weak shopper response. They signal that the unit needs help before discounting becomes the default move.

TurnSignal helps make that difference visible.
Store-Specific Intelligence

Every store is different.

Your store has strong brands and weaker brands. Some units turn fast. Some brands carry more margin. Some categories naturally move slower. That is why TurnSignal uses three years of your store's sales history to improve the signal. It is not trying to force your store into a generic model. It is built to reflect how your store actually performs.

Ghost VDPs

TurnSignal finds zero-traffic Ghost VDPs before they become an aged problem.

These are VINs with abnormally low traffic, abnormally low shopper engagement, cold behavior that signals the page needs help.

Some VDPs do not die all at once. They just go cold. You cannot see that clearly without TurnSignal.

TurnSignal can export Ghost VDP lists to your marketing team so they can get more eyeballs on those units before the store drops the price.

Protect Margin

TurnSignal likes velocity, but uses discounting as a last resort.

Hot VINs with strong starts should not be treated like weak units. Strong first-week and second-week performance matters. TurnSignal helps protect price when the signal says the unit is already doing what it needs to do. It also helps identify when the problem is not price first.

Actionable Reporting

TurnSignal organizes action one stock number at a time.

Layered into the TurnSignal report is your store's sales history. That means each stock number can be reviewed in a way that is store-specific, VIN-specific, and action-oriented. You can decide what to do yourself. Or you can ask TurnSignal to create an organized report with AI-based recommendations that help surface what action makes the most sense next.

TurnSignal FAQ

Do I need all three products?
No. Each product stands on its own. VDP Audit delivers actionable data — missing Wow Factor features, trim mismatches, revenue at risk. How does TurnSignal work?
TurnSignal is unique. We work with your sales data and build your Sales Reach Map — a geographic footprint of where your actual buyers come from. Next, TurnSignal builds your Lead Reach Map — where your leads originate. This study alone is one the industry ignores, but AML is a merchandising company. If you have long-distance leads and few long-distance sales, we’ve got work to do. Next, TurnSignal takes your sales data by year, make, and model — new and used — and reveals what your store loves to sell and what it doesn’t. Add gross profit and age to this report and the data gets very powerful. Take this sales history profile and overlay it on your website engagement data — now you’re seeing supply and demand based on your store’s own profile. All of this connects to what shoppers are doing on your site right now. Three inputs. One picture you’ve never had before.
