Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AutoMagic Labs, VDP Audits, and merchandising intelligence.

The Basics

What is a VDP? ▼

A Vehicle Detail Page — the online listing shoppers see for a specific car on your website or any marketplace (Cars.com, Autotrader, CarGurus). It's where the buying decision happens or doesn't.

What is VDP Pollution? ▼

When a vehicle listing is missing features, has incorrect trim data, or buries high-value options where shoppers can't find them. 37% of VDPs have at least one key feature missing. It doesn't show up on your P&L — it shows up in aging inventory and lost leads.

What is the Treasure Hunter Score™ (THS)? ▼

AML's proprietary scoring engine that ranks every installed vehicle feature across 8 dimensions of shopper value. No competitor scores features at the VIN level. Industry first. The highest-scoring features are called hot features.

Products

What is VDP Boost? ▼

A suite of merchandising tools that take intelligence from the Treasure Hunter Score and put it to work — in photos, descriptions, and every shopper touchpoint. Powered by hot features.

What is TurnSignal™? ▼

AML maps your GA4 data to the actual car shopper journey — per-VIN engagement scoring. When shoppers return to a VDP, that vehicle has heat. Our system measures these return visits to identify which vehicles are driving shopper interest and engagement.

What is Profit Wreck of the Week? ▼

A weekly real-world example of a VDP audit that reveals significant missing value. Real vehicles, dealer names redacted, dollar figures real. It demonstrates VDP Audit in action.
