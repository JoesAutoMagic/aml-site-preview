VDP Audit FAQ

What is a VDP?
A Vehicle Detail Page — the online listing shoppers see for a specific car on your website or any marketplace. It's where the buying decision happens or doesn't.
What data does the audit use?
We cross-reference your VDP listing against factory build data and marketplace listing data. This lets us identify every feature the VIN has that the listing doesn't mention.
How long does it take?
Under 5 minutes for a single VIN. Paste the URL, wait for the decode, and your report is ready. No login or account needed.
What does the grade mean?
A+ through F, scored on feature completeness, trim accuracy, and marketplace consistency. A "C" doesn't mean failure — it means there's specific, fixable revenue hiding in your listing.
Can I audit my whole lot?
The free tool handles one VIN at a time. For full inventory audits, contact us at vdpaudit@automagiclabs.ai — we'll scope a pilot.
Why does this matter for AI shopping?
AI shopping agents compare vehicles by equipped value. An incomplete listing gets ranked below an identical vehicle with a complete VDP. Auditing now means your inventory is ready for both today's shoppers and tomorrow's AI agents.
